<product_requirements>
The product is a GPT Signal Bot, a full-stack web application designed to generate high-accuracy (target 90%+) trading signals for the Pocket Option platform.

**Core Features:**
*   **Web Dashboard:** A React-based frontend featuring bot controls (start/stop, auto-generate), asset selection (Regular and OTC markets), and live signal notifications.
*   **Signal Generation:** A FastAPI backend that uses advanced technical analysis (TA-Lib, pandas), multiple custom trading strategies, and AI/ML models to generate BUY/SELL signals for various timeframes (5s, 15s, 30s, 1m, 3m, 5m).
*   **Force Generate:** A feature to manually trigger signal generation on demand for selected assets.
*   **Precise Timing:** Signal generation must be synchronized with Pocket Option's Chicago Central Time candle formations. A sophisticated latency compensation system is in place to ensure signals arrive at the precise moment for trade execution.
*   **External Integrations:** The system must send generated signals to third-party platforms like AutobotSignal.io and Telegram. A direct Pocket Option trading integration is currently being implemented.

**Recent Implementation Focus:**
Development has been highly iterative, focusing on user-driven requests for new trading strategies, bug fixes (e.g., signal bias, auto-generation logic), performance optimization (e.g., force generate speed), and fine-tuning signal timing to eliminate lag.
